9.4 / 10

"The apotheosis of Hashino's fantasy ambition — with one jarring Act 3 pivot."

Studio Zero took everything that felt rigid in modern Persona calendar loops and applied a pressure cooker of real-time movement and tactical turn-based synthesis. The archetypes aren't just job classes; they are dynamic ideological stances that evolve with your party bonds.

CRITICAL NARRATIVE SECTION:

While the road through the Grand Duchy feels immaculate, the sudden execution of the Elda tribe elder before the final capital parley completely upends the sovereign election mechanics and invalidates your party's pact with the third court. It leaves the endgame feeling emotionally fractured, though the final confrontation restores that breathless Shoji Meguro pulse.
